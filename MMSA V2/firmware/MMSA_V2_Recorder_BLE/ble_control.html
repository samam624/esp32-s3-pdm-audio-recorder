MicdBFSvs basevs othersSNRPhase°
Measures every mic at the tones, recording or not. Save a baseline once the wing is installed, with the wind off. Later checks compare each mic to it. "vs others" removes the change all mics share (speaker level, flow on the sound path), so one mic or hole that shifted stands out: amber above 1 dB, red above 3. Grey rows have SNR under 10 dB: play louder or longer. Every check is also logged to CAL.CSV on the card.
